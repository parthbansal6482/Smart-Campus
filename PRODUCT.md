## Users

1. **Campus Administrators & Operations**: Need high-level visibility across all campus operations (emergency load, classroom utilization, cafeteria dining volume, user roles) with rapid oversight.
2. **Medical Responders & Health Center Staff**: Operating in urgent, high-stakes contexts. Need instant triage of active SOS alerts, patient location pins, ambulance status tracking, pharmacy inventory levels, and doctor callback queue.
3. **Cafeteria Staff & Kitchen Operators**: Fast-paced food prep environment. Need live kitchen order tokens, distinct status progression (Pending → Preparing → Ready → Completed), category filters, and quick availability toggles.
4. **Faculty Members**: Fast room booking, schedule visibility, and IoT classroom environment controls (HVAC/Lights/Projector status).
5. **Students (Mobile App)**: On-the-go campus navigation, seamless room discovery, pre-ordering meals to avoid lines, ordering essential medicine with doorstep delivery, and one-tap emergency SOS.

## Product Purpose

Smart Campus unifies disconnected university services into **One Executive Web Dashboard** for staff and **One Modern Mobile App** for students and faculty. It replaces chaotic paperwork and fragmented departmental portals with an intelligent, real-time command platform that eliminates friction and guarantees campus safety.

Success looks like:
- Responders acknowledge medical emergencies in seconds with zero navigational ambiguity.
- Kitchen staff fulfill dining orders with clear token progression and zero lost tickets.
- Faculty and students book and check room occupancy effortlessly.
- An interface so crisp, responsive, and intuitive that new users require zero training.

## Brand Personality

- **3-Word Persona**: Executive, Pristine, Mission-Critical.
- **Voice & Tone**: Calm authority, razor-sharp clarity, purposeful and quiet confidence.
- **Emotional Goals**: Users feel an immediate sense of trust, control, and efficiency. Critical information jumps out without visual noise.

## Anti-references

- **No AI Slop / Cliché SaaS**: No clownish gradients, no oversaturated decorative glassmorphism, no 32px+ bulbous pill containers, no hand-drawn cartoon doodles.
- **No Washed-Out Low Contrast**: Zero low-contrast pale gray text on white/gray backgrounds. All text must meet crisp readability standards.
- **No Hidden Critical Affordances**: No nesting high-priority status toggles or emergency dispatch actions behind 4 levels of dropdowns or menus.
- **No "Productivity Theater"**: No fake charts, redundant stat cards, or ornamental cards that don't represent real actionable data.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity Precedes Decoration**: Every pixel, badge, and border serves comprehension. If an element doesn't help the operator or student make a faster, better decision, eliminate it.
2. **State Transparency**: Every action (booking confirmed, order token preparing, ambulance dispatched) provides immediate, deterministic visual feedback.
3. **Ergonomic Hierarchy**: High-priority real-time alerts dominate the visual plane; secondary filters and audit logs stay accessible but unobtrusive.
4. **Tactile Precision**: Form controls, buttons, cards, and data rows have deliberate hover, active, focus, and disabled states that feel snappy and dependable.
5. **Unified Design Language**: The web dashboard and mobile app speak the exact same visual dialect—consistent iconography (Lucide), consistent semantic colors, and matching status tokens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG Level**: AA compliance minimum (minimum 4.5:1 text contrast for body copy; 3:1 for large display elements and interactive control borders).
- **Sensory & Motor Accommodations**: Generous touch targets (≥48x48px on mobile, ≥40px on web), explicit focus outlines for keyboard navigation, and zero color-only status indicators (every colored status pill includes an accompanying icon or text label).
- **Reduced Motion**: Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` across both platforms with instantaneous or gentle crossfade transitions.
